Claim Pilot is an enterprise expense claim system built at Daxonet AI. Claimants submit claims through Teams and WhatsApp, or capture documents in a web portal, and each claim passes through claimant, verifier and approver reviews.

Gemini extracts the details from receipts. The backend is FastAPI with a React portal, and the system integrates with accounting and ERP software for invoices and general ledger journals.

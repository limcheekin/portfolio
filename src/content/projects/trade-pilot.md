Trade Pilot is an enterprise order-intake system built at Daxonet AI. It accepts purchase orders, delivery orders and item lists over WhatsApp as messages, images, PDFs and Excel files.

Document extraction feeds a Next.js review portal, and AutoCount and Dynamod integration turns the results into quotations, sales orders and goods received notes. Orders can go through admin review or be processed automatically.
